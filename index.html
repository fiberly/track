Track - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 06/25/2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the Asset Debt and Budget Tracker ("Track" or "the App") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information. Our foundational principle is privacy by design; your financial data is strictly your property.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

All personal information, portfolio details, debts, budgets, financial data, and application settings entered into the App are stored solely and securely stored and encrypted, if using a password on device. We do not host proprietary servers, nor do we collect, process, store, or transmit your personal financial information to any external databases. A name is collected at app first usage, that information is stored in app data.

3. Data Portability and User Control

You retain absolute control over your data. The App includes functionality enabling you to export and transfer your portfolio files to external environments. Please note that upon exporting, the subsequent security, storage, and privacy of those files become your sole responsibility.

4. Third-Party Services and API Integrations

To provide real-time portfolio valuations and currency conversions, the App requires an internet connection strictly to retrieve current market pricing and exchange rates. We integrate with the following third-party APIs for this explicit purpose:

  • Alpaca API (equities and stock pricing) found at https://alpaca.markets/
  • Coinlore (cryptocurrency pricing) found at https://www.coinlore.com/
  • GoldAPI (precious metals pricing) found at https://gold-api.com/
  • ExchangeRate-API (currency conversion and exchange rates) found at https://www.exchangerate-api.com/

During these network requests, the App transmits only the minimum necessary data, such as asset ticker symbols, identifiers, or currency codes. Absolutely no personally identifiable information (PII), transaction histories, or account balances are transmitted to these third-party services. However, your IP address may be exposed during standard network routing. We strongly encourage you to review the individual privacy policies of Alpaca, Coinlore, GoldAPI, and ExchangeRate-API for further details regarding their specific data practices.

5. Analytics and Diagnostic Data

The App strictly prohibits the use of third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising frameworks. Any diagnostic data or crash reports generated are limited to those explicitly authorized and collected via your device's native operating system (e.g., Apple App Store or Google Play Store analytics).

6. Modifications to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at our discretion to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. Any revisions will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this document.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data, please contact us.

8. Contact Information
